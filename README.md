# Swiggy vs Zomato Data Analysis (Excel)

## 📌 Business Problem
Food delivery platforms compete on customer satisfaction, pricing, and user engagement.  
This project analyzes key metrics to compare Swiggy and Zomato and understand differences in customer behavior and platform performance.

---

## 🎯 Objective
To perform a comparative analysis of Swiggy and Zomato using data and identify key factors influencing ratings, engagement, and demand.

---

## 🛠 Tools Used
- Microsoft Excel  
  - Pivot Tables  
  - Data Cleaning  
  - KPI Metrics  
  - Dashboard Creation  

---

## 📊 Dataset Overview
The dataset includes:
- Customer ratings  
- Number of reviews (customer engagement)  
- Average cost per person  
- Estimated order volume  

---

## 📈 Analysis Performed
- Compared average ratings between Swiggy and Zomato  
- Analyzed customer engagement using review counts  
- Evaluated pricing differences (cost per person)  
- Explored relationships between:
- Rating vs Demand (Estimated Orders)  
- Cost vs Rating  
- Built a dashboard to visualize key insights  

---

## 🔍 Key Insights

- Zomato has a slightly higher average rating (~4.0) compared to Swiggy (~3.97), indicating marginally better customer satisfaction  

- Customer engagement is higher on Zomato (average reviews ~2545 vs ~2178), suggesting stronger user interaction  

- The relationship between ratings and demand appears weak, meaning higher ratings do not necessarily lead to significantly more orders  

- Pricing (cost per person) does not show a strong correlation with ratings, indicating customers may not base ratings heavily on price  

---

## 💡 Business Implications

- Platforms can focus on improving **customer engagement strategies** to drive growth  
- Pricing changes may not significantly impact customer ratings  
- Understanding demand behavior can help optimize business strategies

---

## 🚀 Conclusion
This analysis shows that while both platforms perform similarly in ratings, **Zomato leads in customer engagement**, and **customer behavior is not strongly driven by pricing or ratings alone**.
